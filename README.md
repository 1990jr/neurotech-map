# Neurotech Landscape

A sourced sample of neurotechnology organisations around the world. Snapshot: 2026-11-03.

Explore organisations, funding and clinical trials in [the website](index.html). Figures are dated, incomplete and accompanied by source citations. Absence does not imply that an organisation or financing does not exist.

## What changed in this snapshot (2026-11-03)

Organisations: 416, up from 410. Six companies listed on the neurofounders Start-up Map were researched, checked independently and added: ANEUVO, EMVision Medical Devices, Imeka, MYndspan, Rhovica Neuroimaging and Rune Labs.

Verified closed funding rose from USD 6,424,656,032 over 199 counted rounds to USD 6,588,826,949 over 207 counted rounds. Seven of the new rounds belong to the new companies (USD 69,170,917): ANEUVO's Series C, EMVision's IPO, Rhovica's financing round and a grant, and Rune Labs' seed, Series A and strategic rounds. Nyxoah's June 2026 public offering (USD 95,000,000) now counts, because its closing announcement and amount were confirmed on the cited page; the same offering seen at pricing counts once.

Labels: "Source excerpt available" now also requires the excerpt to state the value of a founding year, registration year or funding round. Eight items changed label for that reason. The founding years of Cortechs.ai, Mainstay Medical and Neurable, Gestala's registration year, and uncounted rounds of NeuroXess and OYMotion are now "Provisional"; iMediSync's founding year and an uncounted Evolution Devices award are now "Not yet confirmed". None of these items was counted, so no total changed because of the labels.

Corrections: Gestala's 2025 date is shown as a registration year, not a founding year. SetPoint Medical's 2019 notes are now labelled "Unsupported", because the filing shows money still to be sold; they converted into its counted 2021 round and stay out of the totals. New evidence was added for SetPoint Medical, Subsense and Nyxoah.

Each figure is dated and sourced on its organisation's page. Funding totals remain a floor: rounds not yet found are not included.
