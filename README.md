## What changed in this snapshot (2026-11-18)

Organisations: 417, up from 416. NeuroHarmonics, a UCL spin-out in London developing focused ultrasound neuromodulation, is added. Axoft's record now uses its current website, axoft.us, so links to its page on this site made before this snapshot no longer open it.

Verified closed funding rises from USD 6,693,538,671 over 208 counted rounds to USD 7,277,438,243 over 217 counted rounds. The nine added rounds are Science Corporation's USD 400 million Series D, announced on 17 November 2026; SetPoint Medical's USD 115 million Series D, now matched to its SEC Form D filing, and a USD 27 million round from 2013; and six amounts reported as sold in SEC Form D filings: three for Rune Labs (USD 16.7 million in total) and one each for Nia Therapeutics (USD 23.6 million), NeuroQore (USD 1.5 million) and Fareon (USD 0.1 million). For these six, the amount sold so far counts as a floor, including where the filing shows the offering still open.

Clinical trials: 16 statuses, 6 start dates, one lead sponsor and one title are updated, each checked against the trial's own ClinicalTrials.gov entry. Synchron's profile records that it paused enrolment in trials of its second-generation device, as WIRED reported on 12 November 2026.

Corrections: the Oxford unit is now named Brain Network Dynamics Unit, University of Oxford; ElMindA appears under its current name, Firefly Neuroscience Ltd., linked to its parent Firefly Neuroscience, Inc.; Noctrix Health is shown as acquired by Resmed (completed 1 June 2026); Seer Medical's description now records that the company entered administration around January 2025 and that a new company, Seer Australia Pty Ltd, acquired its assets in July 2025; the Nudge and Firefly descriptions are clarified; six spin-out and acquisition links now open the related organisation; and lead investors or investor lists are corrected on 17 rounds.

Each figure is dated and sourced on its organisation's page. Funding totals remain a floor: rounds not yet found are not included.
