A sourced sample of neurotechnology organisations around the world. Snapshot: 2026-11-08.

## What changed in this snapshot (2026-11-08)

Organisations: 416, unchanged. Verified closed funding is unchanged at USD 6,588,826,949 over 207 counted rounds. No round was added or removed.

Every record was checked again in two passes. Each of the 79 points where a source differed from the record was checked by two independent reviewers. Of these, 57 were settled. The other 22 were left for the owner's decision; two of them (the Theranica trial and the Nexstim clearance) were applied on the owner's go-ahead and are included in this snapshot. Eight records were edited, each on a source-backed change: ABILITY Neurotech's first-in-human procedure for its BCI technology, at TUM University Hospital, is added to its profile and cited to its news page; one clinical trial was added for Theranica and two for BrainGate; Magstim has an FDA 510(k) clearance (K250286, July 2025); Nexstim has an FDA clearance for adult OCD (March 2026, with no 510(k) number on the cited page).

Wording changed for four descriptions: Caltech's brain-machine interface centre, Seer Medical's current status (not for commercial sale, operating status unresolved) and CiNet, which NICT supports and Osaka University co-forms.

The clinical trial count on the site rises from 976 to 979. Each figure is dated and sourced on its organisation's page. Funding totals remain a floor: rounds not yet found are not included.
